## Visual direction

- **Composition:** One portrait design with generous white space. Put the headline at the top, the existing book cover as the central hero, three factual badges beneath it, and the call to action at the bottom. Keep the longer explanation in the caption if it makes the artwork crowded.
- **Style:** Continue the selected futuristic blueprint identity: icy white, cobalt blue, restrained cyan and violet, fine circuit-like paths, and transparent geometric forms.
- **Learning visual:** Beside the cover, show a small connected diagram: documents feed a retrieval block; retrieved context and memory feed an agent; the agent connects to tools through a policy gate; tool observations return to the agent. Place a verification checkpoint before the final output. Make the connections legible rather than adding decorative complexity.
- **Typography:** Space Grotesk for the headline and Source Sans 3 for supporting text. Use a clear hierarchy and strong contrast; keep fine blueprint lines behind, never through, text.
- **Book identity:** Use the approved front cover and exact author name. Avoid a new cover design, invented testimonials, or unverified performance claims.
- **Accessibility:** Suggested alt text: “Agentic AI Engineering by Muhammed Juned Alam, shown beside a diagram connecting knowledge retrieval, memory, an AI agent, governed tools, and verification. The book contains 39 chapters, 287 visuals, and 894 pages.”
