# About the Author

Muhammed Juned Alam is a Principal GenAI Engineer and Graph-RAG Architect with more than ten years of experience designing and delivering enterprise-scale, cloud-native systems. For the past two and a half years he has built production generative-AI platforms for Fortune 500 clients. His work covers retrieval-augmented generation, autonomous agents orchestrated with LangChain and LangGraph, and knowledge-graph engineering with Neo4j, where structured semantic reasoning grounds language models and makes their answers explainable.

At Arabian Control Systems, he leads the GenAI and Graph-RAG architecture of a high-availability groundwater-management platform for a global energy company. The platform combines LLM agents and hybrid knowledge-graph and vector retrieval with real-time sensor data. Before that, he designed and built large-scale platforms in retail e-commerce, national social insurance and pensions, digital signage and network monitoring for clients in the United States and Saudi Arabia, working across Java and Spring, Python, Angular and React, Kafka, Kubernetes, AWS and Azure.

The production lessons in this book come from that work: the refund that must never run twice, the audit trail a regulator will ask for, and the dashboard that has to stay up at peak load. He has also led engineering teams, mentored engineers, and guided organizations through digital transformation. Juned holds a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University, India, and works in English, Hindi and Arabic.
